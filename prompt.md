**Coffee Craft**
- a game about running fine coffee stores in the city (or some other slogan idk, like 'a game of bags and strategy'; maybe come up with a good one from the overall prompt)
- open world, web game, for desktop web and preferably mobile web as well, start with a simple intro modal with the logo, name of the game, a little info about the game, controls, credits, etc., and a play button
- Should have an optional connection to an AI API for certain enhancements of certain features; should probably support Ollama for local installations and also a deployment-friendly alternative offer support for connecting major cloud API providers (should be set in a file e.g. config.php)
- I would like to use local text files (sqlite type of thing) for data persistence, unless a MySQL/MariaDB database is configured in config.php
- Business heavy district, shops with apartment buildings above, a few hotels, some ramen noodle places, some Korean donut places, a convenience store, a drug store, an exotic pet fish store, a pho restaurant, and a several other business; design influence should come from Seattle's International District; should have a park (a sign should note that it is named "Baloo's Park") with benches and tables and trees and an upper and lower level and the lower level should have a mini dog park and there should be a pickleball court--if it's possible to make a simple, fun pickleball mini-game that is playable here (perhaps contending against an AI-powered opponent who you can chat with)
- The map should have a free ride-able streetcar which zigs through the map and has several stops
- The neighborhood should have a "Chinatown gate" or something like that, in the style seen in San Francisco or Seattle, to honor the neighborhood's history
- There should be a big old train station on the map, and maybe it could be one of the stops for the streetcar
- every business is visited by the town's NPCs but they compete on price, buzz, quality, etc.  if the AI API is available, it is used to help refine the behavior and preferences of NPCs.  Businesses all have stats as far as total visitors, community reputation, daily visitors, total revenue, daily revenue, total property assets, total inventory value, total savings, and total debt, also buzz level, more of a vibes metric; when the business hits more debt than total savings + total inventory + total property assets + daily revenue then it is forced into bankruptcy; at that point they can liquidate and leave a vacant lot, or they can have their ownership bought up by the bank--the game has town banks; one is independent and run by the AI API if available and it makes decisions as far as what businesses to make loans to, and what businesses to liquidate vs. buy up and keep running.  The player owns the other bank, this is the mechanism through which they own their coffee shops (and maybe eventually other businesses)
- The Settings page should be styled like a smartphone that you pull up from a button, accessible from the pause menu and from its own smart phone button somewhere like the upper right, and should have tabs for:
  - Account: create account, log in to account; when logged in show name of account; button to allow deletion of account and account data (after warning)
  - Stats: Info about the game and the player's status, maybe hierarchically designed, so that it lists the businesses currently owned, other relevant info
  - Diary: a food diary with names and pictures of the unique food items that the player has consumed
  - Bank: this is the view of the player's bank--its overall finances, holdings, and real-time info for each business.  There should be a checkbox to 'automate' the banking, that way the game (using AI API when possible) makes decisions on lending, bankruptcies (whether to buy out a bankrupt business), liquidation, etc.  if the box is not checked it goes it 'manual' mode where the player is notified (the game needs some kind of notification system in it) about a decision to be made and can optionally click on the notification which should take them to the Settings, where the Bank tab should have a sub-tab which lists the decisions needing to be made and has buttons to decide and compete.
  - TOWS: where available, AI should look at the overall game the player has going and attempt to identify the threats, opportunities, weaknesses, and strengths of their current state
  - Debug: info including Storage type (local JSON, mySQL, or whatever), whether AI chat is enabled, etc.
  - Credits - there should be the game logo and name and it should note "created by [Daniel McKeown](https://altaredwood.work)"
  - Terms (game may be run locally or deployed to web)
  - Privacy Policy
- The player should start with owning two coffee shops and a very large amount of capital.  Since this game is a causal play game I don't want there to be too much 'grinding' (at least in this first experimental phase) so let's start the player out with bank cash holdings somewhere randomly between 10 and 100 times the total asset values of the two coffee shops
- Restaurants and coffee shops are the two main types of businesses that the player owns; after a great amount of time and points accumulation, the donut shop category opens up to being owned and operated as well; other types of businesses can be held as assets
- player should be able to walk in to many places: The restaurants and coffee shops should all have unique menus, which you can view when you sit at a table; they need only 2 to 3 things listed--but each of these can be clicked on and viewed.  The images shown of the dish should come up as a sort of modal, the game should generate the image--best to have fallbacks, but I am wondering if the image could be a simplistic AI-generated picture of the AI API is available?  I am not looking for cutting-edge image creation, just a clever picture of coffee or pho or whatever (maybe an SVG or CSS shape or something).  When the player looks at an image of coffee or food they have the option of buying it with their bank funds, then they are animated enjoying the item if possible 
- Every once in a while a restaurant or coffee shop might have no NPCs, or so many it's hard to find a table, but usually there are a few, having their own conversations; the player should be able to wander over to NPCs and start conversations with them (enhance with AI when the api is available) just like you should be able to talk to most or all the NPCs when out on the town
- cloudy skies many days, sometimes a bit of sun.  the light varies subtly, as with night
- In the settings under the Bank tab there should be another sub-tab called suppliers; this should have a section for coffee and one for restaurants (including donut shops) where the user gets the ability to change suppliers for coffee beans, cups, and payment processor.  Setting the payment processor to 'cash only' adds to risks for businesses but allows the business to add a gambling den which will bring in more cash--but also more scrutiny from the cops and possible effects on your community reputation.  When that gets really low, you are more likely to face harassment from the health board or slow response times with police.  Some NPCs (pretty unusual though) are bad customers and cause problems and you either have to engage them in hand-to-hand combat if you are present (another mini-game or simulation) to get them to go away or you have to wait until the police show up and shoo the problem NPC
- There should be a pause button near the game pad buttons, along with a button to mute the sound; the pause screen should have a button to get to Settings; it should also have a screenshot button to take an image of the current game screen; these screen shots should become visible once the button is pushed, and then have a save button below them; the top or bottom of the screenshot should have this info: "Coffee Craft by Daniel McKeown https://altaredwood.work" and include a timestamp
- One building in town should be the Lab where the player can experiment with new coffee roast if technique (this could be a setting that can be changed for each coffee shop: as the player experiments more with the coffee roasting techniques in the coffee lab, new styles of roasting can become options and this can help increase buzz for the business); the place should have a roasting machine, which when you come up to it and choose to use it you get to a modal where you pick the bean type (from the ones your current coffee shops are using) and then set two or three other knobs so that the blend has some fairly unique signature, then you can sample the coffee and add it to your roasting options at each of your owned coffee shops; the lab should also have an espresso machine to use where you can press buttons and watch the coffee flow into the little espresso mugs
- The NPCs should be somewhat price sensitive and the settings for each coffee shop and restaurant include pricing (maybe a low, medium, high, premium range or something)
- another business in town (owned by NPCs) is the electronics store.  This sells a drone, which when owned adds a special button to the screenshot screen which creates a somewhat zoomed-out overhead view of the game map; they also sell a vintage camera, which when owned adds another button to the screenshot dialogue which creates a black-and-white screenshot
- I have some artworks that should be in the './art' folder of the current working directory that should preferably be an influence on the visual style of the game
- Since time and money are connected, the game should have a subtle sense of time; maybe reflected in the stats but not shown onscreen while playing; to mark nighttime, the tint should be juust a little darker; businesses can set their hours in settings and the neighborhood gets a little quieter at night but it's an urban neighborhood and NPCs are around all the time; there should be a tavern called Striker's Pub where NPCs file out of looking for late night restaurants (it would be cool for the player to be able to go in there and order a beer) there is no particular need to explicitly sleep in the game, but if the player feels the need they can go into the Hotel (named Hotel Bogey), ask the front desk person for a room, and check in and sleep it off.  Maybe the hotel room could be decorated with something from "./art"
- maybe the smartphone should have an app that lists the menus of all the restaurants in the area
- one of the NPCs should be a guy in a hoodie and jeans and a ballcap named Dan, who walks around with a green Quaker parrot on a leash; another NPC should be named Jes, a lovely lady who wears pink outfits, and she walks around with a blue Quaker parrot on a leash; these two would just be two of dozens or more other NPCs; like all NPCs they can get into dialogue and can have their responses created or enhanced by using the AI API

- If the player is running low on funds in their bank, they will see a "bailout" button and when they click it there should be a chance for the player to write the amount they want (up to about 100 times the value of a coffee shop more or less) for a government loan.  When the loan would come up for interest payments, the player should get a notification "your loan has been paid off by the government" and they don't actually ever have to pay these back
- The smartphone should have an App Downloader app, which currently just has the apps already on the phone but could have more apps in later versions
- There should be a few raccoons and pigeons and seagulls wandering around, getting into the trash cans and dumpsters in the alleys and stuff like that
- the neighborhood should have a flow of cars, buses, cycles, motorcycles, and scooters along the roads.  There should be parking spaces along the street in many areas, so it should have the crowded urban feel: lots of NPCs using the spots and looking for parking.  The player should be able to use scooters as they are a city program and free to all; I would like for the cars and buses to be curvy in shaped and if possible have a kind of "tilt-shift" effect on them to make them look sort of miniature (but still scaled correctly for the game)
- would like the project to have a developer-friendly README, with info about both running the game locally and deploying it to the web, also includes some info about installing Ollama and getting it running, explain how to use the config.php file (maybe use the config.local.php pattern to have a deployable file that is .gitignore-ed)
- The player should be a woman by default, professionally dressed for her role running the businesses and checking into them during the workday, it should be a third person view game
- The game should have a few sounds for eating food, getting in and out of scooters, using the coffee roasters, stuff like that; the game should also have generated music in the background, fairly light, ambient, abstract stuff, punctuated every once in a while by a change to a trance track or two (maybe when it rains in the game?) before transitioning back to mellow and trancey.
- The smartphone could have a streaming music app that allows the user to to mute the music or change it to 2 other channels (other than the ambient trance default one)--a classical stream that has generated music with violins and all that stuff and maybe a zither once in a while, and a white noise channel
- there should be a number of different types of coffee shops in terms of their style and presentation (indie comfortable-couch joints, Vietnamese coffee stands, robot barista places, corporate style quick service, diners with coffee, and more)
- The art style should be influenced by mid-90s polygon graphics--so it should have that sort of 3d vibe, but it's ok if it has some long straight edges; the neighborhood is urban modern Pacific Northwest so it should look a little damp, with a handful of murals with Sasquatches and whales on them; this is a pretty gritty area, for this city, so we want alleys behind businesses, a derelict vacant building here and there, and a library where their only book available currently is `Kusamakura` by Soseki Natsume (if that book's sensibility were to influence this game, that would be for the better).  I would like the colors in this game to use plenty of green and yellow, with gray, brown, and blue mixed in, and not totally avoiding red but mostly using it in emphatic situations or scenes, preferring orange for many reddish hues.
