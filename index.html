Coffee Craft

A game of bags, beans and bad weather

Run two coffee shops in a damp, gritty, beloved city neighborhood — ramen counters, Korean donuts, a streetcar, an old train station and a park with a pickleball court. Your bank funds your shops; an independent bank competes with it. Wander, talk to the neighbors, drink something good.
